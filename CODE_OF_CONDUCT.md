# Code of conduct

Sentinel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and welcome people of every background and experience level. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated, in the repository or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), starting the title with "Conduct". Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so repeatedly or severely.
